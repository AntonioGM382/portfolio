Document workflow automation

JPAInf OCR

Batch PDF ingestion and structured extraction through Azure Computer Vision, replacing document-heavy manual entry with a repeatable pipeline.

01 · Ingest

Validate and process PDF batches consistently, with a clear input boundary for retryable work.

02 · Recognize

Send documents to Azure Computer Vision and collect page, line, and text results.

03 · Structure

Transform OCR output into JSON and text that downstream business processes can consume.

Operational outcome

The separation between ingestion, recognition, and extraction makes the workflow inspectable, retryable, and ready for business-process automation.
