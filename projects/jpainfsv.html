JPAINFSV

A modular C# Windows service for system visibility, automated detection, and watchdog recovery.

Service architecture

Modular visibility

Monitoring, diagnostics, logging, and recovery remain separate concerns around a coordinated service loop.

  • Tracks machine resources.
  • Detects unhealthy services.
  • Automates common recovery paths.

Repeatable delivery

PowerShell automation handles installation, registration, configuration, and restart operations consistently.

Operational outcomes

Observable checks

Recurring health signals become visible system events.

Fast recovery

Watchdog paths reduce manual response time.

Consistent rollout

Deployment scripts make service operations repeatable.
