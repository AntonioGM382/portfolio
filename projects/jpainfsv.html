Windows operations automation

JPAINFSV

A modular C# Windows service for system visibility, automated error detection, and watchdog recovery.

Modular architecture

MonitoringService.cs coordinates the service loop while keeping resource tracking, diagnostics, logging, and recovery concerns separate.

  • Tracks machine and system resources.
  • Detects unhealthy or stopped services.
  • Automates the watchdog path instead of relying on manual intervention.

Repeatable deployment

PowerShell automation handles installation, configuration, registration, and restart operations consistently across environments.

  • Less machine-by-machine setup.
  • Auditable deployment behavior.
  • Faster recovery from operational interruptions.

Business value

JPAINFSV protects developer and operations flow by converting repetitive checks into a dependable service with a clear operational response.
