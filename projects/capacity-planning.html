Technical product management and operational strategy

Turning a fragmented enterprise process into an adopted, measurable operating system.

The enterprise problem

More than ten fragile, disconnected Google Sheets created inconsistent formulas, duplicated effort, and limited traceability across European operations. The platform replaces that sprawl with one governed source of truth and a workflow designed around real operational decisions.

Scale and multi-stakeholder workflows

Over 1,500 employees self-service schedules and vacations. Dedicated capacity planners calibrate plans, regional managers compare coverage, and country administrators apply custom constraints without breaking the shared model.

Unit economics and target calibration

Formula pricing uses 20 and 30 minute evaluation intervals. Mobile route profitability accounts for travel distances and expenses, linking staffing decisions to operational economics rather than headline capacity alone.
