Engineering foundation

People, roles, branches, rosters, skills, contracts, calendars, shifts, leave, assignments, optimization runs, recommendations, and outcomes are separated into a normalized MySQL model.

Historical snapshots preserve the exact roster, target, and rule inputs used for each planning decision.

M3 optimization

Dynamic programming models reachable assignments and time windows. Travel allowance matrices, reach maps, role fit, coverage, and operational penalties guide the solution.

Protected operations

Vacation, half day leave, bank holidays, paid time off, and temporary reassignments remain explicit statuses. Conflicts become explainable outcomes rather than overwritten data.
