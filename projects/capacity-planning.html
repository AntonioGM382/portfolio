Enterprise workforce scheduling · confidential case study

Capacity Planning Tool

An enterprise-grade platform for a major European automotive marketplace, replacing fragmented branch spreadsheets with governed, auditable scheduling.

Next.js 16React 19TypeScriptDjango 6MySQL 8.4AWS ECS Fargate

The problem

European branches were coordinating people, shifts, leave, and coverage in disconnected spreadsheets, creating duplicate work, conflicting versions, slow replanning, and weak cross-region visibility. The goal was not just digitization: it was one trusted operational model that local managers could use and leadership could measure.

Architecture and state

Next.js 16 App Router static export, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind, TanStack Query, Django 6, and a 38-table normalized MySQL 8.4 relational model run in Docker on AWS ECS Fargate. Complex state machines cover availability, leave, requests, permissions, assignments, and outcomes.

Master versus live scheduling

Shift is the reusable master template. StagingShift is the live calendar. A six-month forward replication applies business rules rather than blindly overwriting data.

MASTER SHIFT → 6-MONTH REPLICATION → LIVE STAGING SHIFTS

Vacation, leave, half-day, holiday, and temporary statuses remain protected, so planners can improve future patterns without erasing operational commitments.

Optimization and PM rigor

M3 mobile workforce optimizer

A dynamic-programming core with swap refinement searches feasible assignments while travel allowances, reach maps, and regional constraints keep results operationally useful.

Vacation-cover engine

A loss-minimization model ranks cover candidates against the cost of uncovered capacity. Historical calibration compares recommendations with realized outcomes, producing measurable technical-PM feedback without exposing proprietary records.

Impact

The system reduces reconciliation, shortens replanning cycles, preserves exceptions, and makes staffing decisions explainable through consistent metrics and auditable data.

Confidentiality boundary

Company names, internal links, private domains, file names, credentials, and operational identifiers are intentionally omitted. The architecture and engineering decisions remain representative of the work.
